# PFRDA Grade A - Complete Discussion Summary

## Exam Pattern (Current 2026)

### Phase 1 (Qualifying) - 2 Papers
- **Paper 1**: English (20Q, 25M), Quant (20Q, 25M), Reasoning (30Q, 25M), GA (20Q, 25M) = 90 Qs, 100 marks, 60 min
- **Paper 2**: General Stream Professional Knowledge = 50 Qs, 100 marks, 40 min
- Negative marking: ¼ per question
- Separate cut-offs per paper + aggregate

### Phase 2 (Merit - 85%) - 2 Papers
- **Paper 1**: Descriptive English (Essay, Precis, Comprehension) = 100 marks, 60 min
- **Paper 2**: Stream-specific MCQs = 50 Qs, 100 marks, 40 min
- Negative marking: ¼ per question (Paper 2 only)

### Phase 3 (Interview - 15%)
- Combined with Phase 2 for final merit

## Repeated Question Themes (High Probability)

1. **Companies Act**: Section numbers (CSR, AGM, directors' duties, NCLT), Chapter-wise provisions
2. **Pension Sector**: NPS architecture (CRA, POP, PRAN), APY eligibility, Vatsalya 2025, EPS-95 vs NPS
3. **Costing**: BEP formula, margin of safety, material/labour variances, standard costing
4. **Finance**: WACC, CAPM, NPV/IRR, money market instruments, regulatory bodies
5. **Accountancy**: Ratio analysis, Ind AS vs AS, cash flow (direct/indirect)
6. **Economics**: IS-LM shifts, multiplier calculation, Phillips curve, BoP components
7. **Management**: Leadership styles, Maslow/Hertzberg, communication barriers, MBO process
